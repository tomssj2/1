A Family Affair

Funky Cow is a small, family-owned spot in the heart of Naousa. We fell in love with the classic American diner — the red booths, the checkered floors, the sizzle of the flat-top — and decided our town needed one of its own.

We keep it simple: quality beef smashed fresh to order, real ice cream shakes, and a warm welcome for everyone who walks through the door. Pull up a stool — you're part of the family now.
